During my MSc at the [South African Astronomical Observatory](https://www.saao.ac.za/) (SAAO) in Cape Town, from 2021 to 2023, I did extensive public outreach.

## An outreach volunteers club

I formed an outreach volunteers club at SAAO. Together, we helped out with the observatory's weekly open nights and with site tours of the 200-year-old observatory, which began in 1820 as the Royal Observatory at the Cape of Good Hope.

## Looking after the outreach telescopes

We also cleaned the outreach telescopes and started maintaining them better.

## Talks and writing

I gave an outreach talk, and as a volunteer with Astronomers for Planet Earth I was one of many contributors to an [article in the *Communicating Astronomy with the Public* journal](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2021CAPJ...30...28F/abstract) (2021).

## Related

At SAAO, I also founded [Green SAAO](/sideprojects/greensaao/), a sustainability movement at the observatory.
