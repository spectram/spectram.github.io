During my MSc at the South African Astronomical Observatory (SAAO) in Cape Town, I formed an outreach volunteers club. Together, we helped out with the observatory's weekly open nights and with site tours of the 200-year-old observatory.

We also cleaned the outreach telescopes and started maintaining them better. I also gave an outreach talk.
